# POPPING VISUALS — THE MV PLAYBOOK

**What this is.** The build-ready distillation of everything the 2026-11-15
research volley banked — nine lilypad creator studies (Kurupuri, surreailist,
Taketo Muroi, niceaunties, Doopiidoo, Allanweird, TechHalla, Olga Fedorova,
Mystique Licorne), the esoteric-AI-video attention report, the studio Event
Library + Winners Canon — folded into the standing house law of the
Slopcannon skill. Written for the test build: when the test track lands, this
is the document the coordinator runs down before volley 1.

**Precedence.** This playbook EXTENDS house law; it never overrides it. The
song is the tempo gospel (video warps to song, never reverse). Word-sync,
G7 wrong-lyric gate, MOTION LAW, the event doctrine, ONE 16:9 master, and
FRAME 0 = PEAK IMAGE all stand exactly as written in the Slopcannon skill.
Where a study technique conflicts with house law, the conflict is named and
house law wins (see the dose note and the freeze reconciliation in §2).

**Why any of this works (the one paragraph to remember).** Attention is
grabbed by *prediction error*: a scene-inconsistent thing inside an instantly
legible scene is fixated earlier, longer, and remembered better — disgust-
textured wrongness registers in ~100–200 ms, before caption or narrative can
(esoteric-attention report §4A/B, van Hooff 2013; Allegretti & Coco 2023).
Morbid curiosity is reward-linked (Oosterwijk 2020; Scrivner 2021), but
recreational fear is an inverted-U: threatening enough to hold, obviously
artificial enough not to repel (Andersen 2020). Every rule below is one of
three moves: **make frame 0 a prediction error, keep the error unresolved,
dose it so it stays playful.**

---

## THE RANKED LAWS OF POP (top of the playbook — if you read nothing else)

1. **Frame 0 is already wrong.** No "before" shot, no settle, no
   establishing drift. The opening frame is the peak image with the
   transformed/wrong state already in it, in motion. (House banger test +
   START ALREADY TRANSFORMED.)
2. **One impossible thing per frame, in a boring-correct world.** Exactly
   one boundary fails — one surface, one scale lie, one invasion. Everything
   else obeys physics, light, and shadow, or the eye has nothing to verify
   against and the grab dies. (Muroi's precondition; Fedorova's scale rule.)
3. **The event is the scene.** Every slot carries one named event
   (start → peak → payoff, peak on a named beat/word), distinct within the
   video, checked against the used-events ledger. A subject at a place is
   an atmosphere, not a scene. (Event doctrine.)
4. **Cut on the peak, never the settle.** Transformations land their cut on
   the morph's peak; joints land 2–4 frames before a motion peak; the first
   cut lands ≤2.5 s in. A morph left unresolved at the cut is legal — the
   open information gap is the hook.
5. **Dose the grotesque.** Obviously artificial, played deadpan, nobody on
   screen surprised. The middle of the inverted-U is where the views are.
6. **Prompt beats, not situations.** Every vehicle describes every beat of
   the action from the first frame ("correct, nice looking, and too calm"
   is the failure this kills), leads with the event sentence, and carries a
   per-project BAN LIST so the model improvises only where we let it.
7. **Still first on hero slots.** The peak frame exists as a judged still
   before motion money is spent; the video prompt then carries behaviour,
   not world. (niceaunties' 25–30 prompts/video, moved upstream.)
8. **Joints are designed, not found.** Portal chains, fog seams, gesture
   seams, pose-cuts, 1-second overlaps — every transition in the plan names
   its mechanism before volley 1.
9. **Density is scored, not felt.** Energy arc in cuts/minute per section;
   montage spans ≥5 s carry ≥3 collision types per 10 s; the final chorus
   is the densest section and holds the visual peak; no single vantage
   held >10 s. (G6 counted rules.)
10. **Texture is a finish, applied once, everywhere.** One grain/texture
    profile across the whole cut (post texture pass, grain-toggle test) —
    and intentional irregularity is never polished away in a later pass.

## 1. FIRST SECOND — the opening-frame recipe (fire-time checklist)

The intro slot is the whole game: 29% of feed swipes land in the first
fifth of a video, and the grab mechanisms fire in 100–200 ms. Build S01
to this checklist, in order:

- [ ] **Legible space first.** Frame 0's setting reads in one glance —
      office, kitchen, street, pool, hallway. If the viewer can't name the
      place instantly, the violation can't register instantly.
- [ ] **The wrongness is already in frame 0.** Transformed state, liquid
      wall, wrong scale, flesh invasion — present on contact, in motion.
      Never open normal and transform later (that spends the first seconds
      on setup; the gag-transformation's setup→turn shape is for *later*
      slots, not S01).
- [ ] **Exactly one wrong thing.** Count the impossibilities in the frame.
      If it's more than one, restage. The singer/character stays solid and
      correct — they are the viewer's verification anchor.
- [ ] **The wrongness touches something trusted.** The failing surface
      meets the floor, stands behind the singer, crosses a thirds line —
      the disagreement happens at a contact line the eye checks
      automatically. Wrongness floating in empty space doesn't freeze
      anyone. (THE LIQUID WALL placement rule.)
- [ ] **Peak image + motion event + mouth.** Carry element visible, a
      motion event running, open mouth if the intro is sung. Frame 0 must
      pass the muted 3-second test: motion visible, carry visible, mouth
      open if sung — with the sound off.
- [ ] **High-energy event, already moving.** S01 draws a High-energy event
      from the library (intro slots need High) and it is *in progress* at
      frame 0 — mid-flight, mid-morph, mid-eruption. A take that starts
      still and then moves is the halted-photo failure, a QC fail.
- [ ] **First cut ≤2.5 s**, on the first downbeat or first word onset,
      whichever is first — and the incoming shot lands 2–6 frames *before*
      its downbeat (picture leads the beat).
- [ ] **No text, no title, no caption in frame.** LoopHole bans in-video
      titles for chain continuity; house law bans generated text outright.
      Kinetic type is a post layer and never carries the first-second hook —
      the image does all the work.
- [ ] **Dose check.** Is the threat obviously artificial / deadpan? If a
      fresh viewer would flinch *away* rather than lean in, soften the
      texture, keep the concept. (Inverted-U: the middle dose wins.)

**The three proven S01 shapes** (pick one per build, name it in the plan):
(a) **THE LIQUID WALL opener** — photoreal ordinary place, one load-bearing
surface already behaving liquid at frame 0, singer solid against it.
(b) **Start-already-transformed** — the figure/object is mid-metamorphosis
or fully transformed on contact (Kurupuri's flesh-invaded office; the
balloon head that simply *is*). (c) **DEADPAN SCALE** — one everyday object
at architectural scale in a mundane setting, cast treating it as normal
(niceaunties' ramen-bowl bathtub; Fedorova's massive hands).

## 2. PER-SLOT POP — event selection + the new forms, keyed to song section

**Base procedure (unchanged house law).** Map the song → assign every slot
ONE distinct event from the Event Library (64 events, 12 families) → check
USED_EVENTS.md (an event spent in either of the last two MVs needs a
materially different staging or a different event; signature-tier 1.1, 1.6,
3.1, 6.1, 10.5 never in consecutive MVs) → write the event sentence FIRST
in every vehicle → the QC event-read test ships one honest sentence per
scene ("the singer sings at [place]" = fail). The forms below are the new
ammunition for that procedure — when each is the right tool, by section.

### INTRO (S01)
- **THE LIQUID WALL** (Muroi) — the glitch peak-frame. Recipe: visually
  complete photoreal scene, NO glitch vocabulary in the prompt (no
  datamosh/pixel/RGB-split — textural brokenness is filed as "broken
  image" and kills the effect); exactly one solid behaving liquid
  (rippling wall, meniscus floor, sagging door); failure placed on the
  eye's checkpoint line; subject solid. The wrongness is *semantic, not
  textural*.
- **Deadpan flight / elemental body** (library 3.1 / 1.6, signature-tier,
  rationed) — when the track's first line wants a body event instead of a
  place event. Start already rising / already elemental.
- **THE SOFT THREAT** (surreailist) — the slow register for a quiet intro:
  a caring/sacred/beautiful gesture already slightly wrong at frame 0,
  held one beat past comfort. No jump, no climax. For ballad-tempo
  openers where High-energy would fight the song.

### HOOK / CHORUS (the densest sections — 40–66 cuts/min, final chorus ≥1.5× verse density, visual peak lives here)
- **Gag-transformation** (Kurupuri, manzai timing) — THE chorus weapon for
  transformation events: mundane setup held just long enough to read as
  normal → the turn → the morph as punchline, cut ON the peak. Chorus
  slots are where the setup can be shortest because the hook is already
  known — compress setup, spend frames on the peak.
- **Morph chain** (library 1.1, signature-tier, one per video max) — the
  final chorus's peak slot is its natural home.
- **Multiplication & crowd events** (Families 4 & 9 — clones arrive, the
  many move as one; canon: "The Real Slim Shady" factory, "Not Like Us"
  street crowd) — chorus scale-up: the hook performed by more bodies than
  the verse had.
- **DEADPAN SCALE** (niceaunties / Fedorova) — the wrong-size prop or
  giant-in-small-world (2.1/2.3) played straight; the cast's non-reaction
  is the comedy and the grab.
- **Pose-as-cut density** — chorus cutting rides pose changes (pose-cut,
  LOOK BLOCK rule): at each boundary the pose changes while the world
  changes around it; the cut hides in the pose change.

### VERSE (12–20 cuts/min — colour, story, the singer in the world)
- **PHRASE-LENGTH TAKES** (Doopiidoo) — word-sync stays the fine grid for
  sung spans, but non-sung verse spans are planned in WHOLE PHRASES of
  the measured track: span = integer phrase count, cuts preferred at
  phrase boundaries where no word anchor forces them, a morph that needs
  room gets the whole next phrase instead of a squeeze-warp.
- **THE SYNOPSIS POEM before the prompts** (Doopiidoo) — for every
  non-sung span, write its 3–5-sentence myth first (including how it
  ends — the residue), generate prompts FROM the poem, and QC the take
  against the poem as well as the grid.
- **Visual puns** (Family 11 — the lyric made literal for one beat) and
  **prop comes alive** (1.4) — verse colour events; low repeat-risk,
  different prop = new event.
- **THE DECOY READER** (Allanweird) — in two-person verse shots, the
  second figure performs a tiny absorbing non-periodic action (reading a
  label, watching a fly) and never reacts to the grotesque. Owns the
  first fixation, plays the deadpan beat, camouflages seams.
- **THE SECTION CUT** (niceaunties) — for a verse establishing beat:
  compose the still as an architectural section, environment cut open,
  2–3 levels of action legible in one frame. A whole location's story in
  a 1–2 s beat that still reads at feed speed.
- **THE STERILE ROOM** (Fedorova) — the dread verse register: aseptic
  near-empty clinically lit space + one too-still figure + exactly one
  absurd prop. Prediction error by subtraction — the zero-gore pole.
- **THE READY-MADE FIGURE** (Fedorova) — for cast members who read as a
  category (the NPC, the auntie, the mannequin): hold them by type + 2–3
  signature props + finish, not by fragile face-lock. Survives refires
  and generator swaps that break identity lock. (Named singers still get
  the reference stack — this is for category figures.)

### BRIDGE / BREAK (15–25 cuts/min — a TEXTURE BREAK, never just more of the same slower)
House law: the bridge is a texture break — location, colour, speed, or
one unbroken take — from a different event family than both choruses.
The studies hand the bridge its best tools:
- **THE FOG SEAM tableau** (surreailist) — one cyclical ambient mover
  (fog, rain, breathing, candle flicker) carries a held tableau; the
  scene never finishes happening, so it loops and breathes. Motion-law
  compliant (it IS moving from frame 0) and the natural bridge register
  for dread. Loop closure by construction, no seam engineering.
- **THE VERIFICATION FREEZE** (Muroi, timing companion) — when a glitch
  peak-frame lands, hold it a beat longer than the cut rhythm wants
  (6–12f suspension *inside* motion, or at the end of a moving take) so
  the viewer's stalled recognition completes. Reconciliation with the
  MOTION LAW: the hold never opens a take and is never a dead still —
  it is a suspension within a moving span. Flagged in the plan when used.
- **THE PLACE BECOMES THE CREATURE** (Doopiidoo) — the location itself is
  the event: the room/street/fridge interior watches, breathes, digests,
  wakes, while the humans stay ordinary. Nothing transforms — the place
  was alive the whole time. The bridge's non-singing subject.
- **THE LENTICULAR CUT** (Fedorova) — one tableau, two states; flip
  between them on a beat or a camera move. The transition IS the event;
  nothing moves in between. Generator-free aliveness for a held shot.
- **THE VISUALIZER CUT** (Mystique Licorne) — where no mouth is owed:
  worlds that evolve and flow with the track's energy curve, no
  narrative burden. Cut world-evolution to the energy curve, not to an
  event the slot doesn't need.
- **THE KEYHOLE** (surreailist, framing discipline) — plan the bridge (and
  any bumper) as a keyhole glimpse of a larger unseen film: the plan
  names the unseen film in one line and shows only the projection on the
  wall. A series of slots from the same unseen film accumulates into a
  mythology none of them has to explain.

### OUTRO / TAIL
- **THE LIFECYCLE ARC** (Doopiidoo) — created → worshiped → destroyed →
  laughed at → forgotten → residue, compressed into the tail slot; end
  on the material remainder (the puddle, the ash, the empty pedestal)
  held as the last image. The residue frame is the memory hook — and it
  rhymes with frame 0 per house law (final frame rhymes with the first).
- **Fog-seam loop-close** — if the video should feel loopable, let the
  tail's ambient mover match the intro's, so the end re-enters the start.

### Cross-section rules (every slot)
- **ONE COHERENT ELSEWHERE** (Kurupuri + niceaunties) — the plan names
  the single elsewhere its portals/transformations open onto; every take
  is a grab from the same place. Montage may jump worlds; it may not
  jump *logics*.
- **Motif returns ≥3 times**, staged as a different event or an explicit
  escalation each time — never a re-run.
- **STILL-FIRST SLOT on hero slots** (niceaunties) — S01, chorus peaks,
  chain first-frames: still brief → generate still → eye-gate it as a
  finished frame (composition legible in one glance, event readable,
  cast correct) → animate with a behaviour-only prompt. Accidents at the
  still stage are harvested: a better unexpected detail re-briefs the
  slot before motion money is spent. File every still, used or not —
  the library seeds the next project's reference stack.
- **Crew-role discipline** (surreailist's credit block) — one tool, one
  film-department job, named in the plan: scenography (still/reference),
  camera (video), soundtrack (the Suno track). A tool asked to do two
  jobs badly is a quiet failure mode; per-piece swaps stay legible.

## 3. TRANSITIONS + TEXTURE — the between-slot craft

### Transitions (name the mechanism in the plan, per joint)
- **THE LOOPHOLE portal chain** (Kurupuri / LoopHole grammar) — a take
  OPENS with an arrival (falls/drops/enters from above) and CLOSES with
  an exit through a visible portal (hole, door, screen, mouth, drain)
  that the next take's world sits behind. The joint is a designed EVENT,
  so the chain survives world changes that break frame-matching.
  **Universal-joint heads and tails:** one standard entry state + one
  standard exit state across a chain makes takes interchangeable — a
  replacement-pack take only has to satisfy the joint contract.
- **THE RADIANT PORTAL** (Mystique Licorne) — the portal is the scene's
  light source: the exit in world N is lit by the portal's glow, the fall
  into world N+1 is lit by the same glow arriving. The transition carries
  its own motivated light across the cut (satisfies the LOOK BLOCK's one
  motivated light at the joint, where light usually breaks).
- **Last-frame → first-frame chaining** (house CONTINUITY method) —
  resume-chaining first choice, two-image chain the fallback; fresh first
  frames allowed when they line up with the outgoing frame's world.
  Never chain from a rejected take.
- **THE GESTURE SEAM** (Allanweird) — for looped segments and bumpers:
  compose a mechanically periodic carrier gesture (rocking, brushing,
  stirring, breathing; period 2–4 s), cut mid-cycle at its low-velocity
  neutral pose, finish the join in the edit (matched-frame cut or 2–6f
  dissolve at the neutral pose), and offset the audio seam from the
  video seam. Acceptance: a fresh viewer can't point to the loop moment
  within ±0.5 s across 5 loops; no background jump or luminance pop at
  the join frame. Stop asking the generator for a perfect loop — the
  edit finishes it.
- **THE FOG SEAM** — ambient-mover joints: when both sides of a cut share
  one cyclical ambient mover, the cut hides inside the fog/rain/flicker.
- **Whip pan + staged morph** (house) — the carry crosses the lens or
  passes behind the subject across the 0.5–1.0 s morph; cut ON the morph
  landing, never hold the end-anchor frame.
- **THE 1-SECOND OVERLAP** (TechHalla) — when chunking a song span for
  generation, cut audio chunks starting 1 s before the previous chunk
  ends and stitch on the overlap.
- **Motion match at every joint** (house) — cuts land 2–4f BEFORE a
  motion peak in the outgoing take; the incoming take's first 6 frames
  carry motion in the same screen direction or a matched shape/position.
  Vehicles name their exit motion ("ends mid-reach / mid-turn /
  mid-crest") so assembly has motion to cut on.

### Montage density (the turnover craft)
- **Collision quota:** every montage span ≥5 s contains ≥3 distinct
  collision types per 10 s — scale change, angle change, world/location
  change, graphic match, action match, light/tone flip. Metric-only
  cutting (uniform shot lengths regardless of content) FAILS G6.
- **Peak turnover:** at the visual peak, approach ~1 s per world (the
  dreamcore montage benchmark: 14 scenes in 14 s). A new impossible image
  lands before the previous one can be categorised — but each image
  still obeys one-wrong-thing; density is turnover, not stacking.
- **Sledgehammer benchmark** (Winners Canon): a new micro-event every
  few seconds across a whole video is the density the canon remembers.
  Counterweight: **the micro-event** ("Nothing Compares 2 U" — one held
  close-up, a single tear) — density is a chorus tool; one held frame
  with one tiny event is a legal bridge/outro register, once per video.

### Texture (the finish)
- **POST TEXTURE PASS** (house) — after grade/match: fine monochrome
  grain weighted to midtones/shadows, fraction-of-a-stop vignette,
  saturation −10–15%, blacks lifted slightly — the SAME profile on every
  clip. **Grain-toggle test:** toggle the pass off; if the frame doesn't
  suddenly look plastic, the grain is too light or absent.
- **Texture wrongness as a deliberate finish** (esoteric §2.6) — grain,
  VHS, flicker can be the look (Dor Brothers' grit began as error-cover
  and became signature; Cuerpa Mostra kept AnimateDiff flicker as "the
  defect becomes the form"). Deliberate and uniform, or not at all —
  accidental per-clip texture variance reads as a defect, not a style.
- **KEEP THE FRICTION** (Allanweird, SAM §10 + the NF_IX annulment) —
  when a regeneration or polish pass smooths away a mark, texture, or
  asymmetry that was deliberately put there, annul the round, keep the
  rougher version, and log the annulment in the QC record. A pass that
  erases intentional irregularity is a defect, not an improvement.
- **THE LIGHTING BLOCK, verbatim** (Mystique Licorne) — write the
  lighting block ONCE per video ("Lighting is identical to…"), paste it
  unchanged into every vehicle; any per-take rewording of the light is
  a drift risk. Light is held by copy-paste discipline, not re-description.

## 4. ANTI-POP LIST — what reliably reads flat (banned by evidence)

From the studies and our own rejected builds. Each item has shipped or
been published somewhere and failed; none is a matter of taste.

1. **The halted-photo start.** Take opens on a still, holds, bursts,
   stops. MV-11 v1 passed every internal gate and was rejected on
   playback ("the same image and then some 4 seconds of splash gen").
   Stop-start flow is a QC fail, full stop.
2. **The singing-at-camera spine.** A held vantage of a face singing at
   the lens as the video's structure (MV-8 v2 rejection). Singing lives
   in the world; the mouth is one instrument among many.
3. **Bland and repeated events.** MV-12/13/14 all spent the same two
   mechanics (glowing carry-object + world-snap montage) and read bland
   for it — the finding that created the event doctrine. Same event
   twice in one video, or the last video's event unre-staged, is flat
   by definition.
4. **The situation prompt.** "A house rolls down a road" generates
   "correct, nice looking, and too calm" (TechHalla's round-one
   failure). Prompt every beat of the action from the first frame, or
   the take arrives pre-flattened.
5. **Textural glitch as wrongness.** Datamosh, pixel smear, RGB split:
   the brain files it as "broken image" in one step and there is no
   freeze, no grab (Muroi's anti-lift). Wrongness must be semantic —
   a thing behaving wrong in a correct world.
6. **Stacked impossibilities.** Three wrong things in one frame cancel
   out: no anchor, no verification, no freeze. One invasion per slot.
7. **Explanation.** Captions, titles, or setup shots that explain the
   weirdness before or after it lands. The scene's winners are
   caption-less (Kurupuri) or title-only (surreailist); explanation is
   exiled to the delivery message and the project record, never the
   frame. The work withholds; the viewer's cognition does the work.
8. **Metric-only cutting.** Uniform shot lengths regardless of content;
   cutting on every beat for a whole section. Rhythm is tension →
   release: hold a shot across bars to build tension, then snap back.
9. **The mouth-lie cut.** Singer change mid-phrase, or a cut to a closed
   or wrong mouth under a sung line. A named G6 fail, not a nit.
10. **The default look.** Anything the model produces when a dimension
    is left unspecified: generic AI iconography (brains, robots, neural
    nets, sparkles), purple/blue neon, glassmorphism, soft gradients,
    bouncy easing (TechHalla's ban list). If the project has no written
    BAN LIST, the defaults are the look.
11. **The over-polished round.** A refire that smooths away the
    deliberate roughness, asymmetry, or texture that made the take read
    (KEEP THE FRICTION). Polish is not a direction.
12. **The world that melts mid-take.** Text-first generation re-casting
    or re-building the world inside a take (the failure STILL-FIRST
    exists to kill on hero slots) — and its cousin, the anchor-still
    head: a take that opens on the reference image held still (see #1).

## 5. PRE-FLIGHT CHECKLIST — run down before firing volley 1 of the test build

**Plan gate**
- [ ] Timeline measured from THIS delivered track (sections, word grid,
      phrase boundaries marked) — never a uniform grid, never another
      master's timings.
- [ ] Energy arc written per section in cuts/minute (verse 12–20,
      pre-chorus 20→40, chorus 40–66, bridge 15–25, outro 10–15); final
      chorus is densest and holds the visual peak; every section names
      its tension and its release.
- [ ] Every slot has ONE named event (library number or named study
      form), distinct within the video; USED_EVENTS checked for the last
      two MVs; signature-tier rationing respected; montage rows name
      happenings, not moods.
- [ ] ONE COHERENT ELSEWHERE named in one line at the top of the plan.
- [ ] S01 passes §1's checklist on paper: legible space, wrongness at
      frame 0, exactly one wrong thing, contact line, High-energy event
      in motion, first cut ≤2.5 s.
- [ ] Bridge is a texture break from a different family than both
      choruses; its form is named (fog seam / freeze / place-creature /
      lenticular / visualizer / keyhole).
- [ ] Motif named, returning ≥3 times as escalation or new event;
      final frame rhymes with the first.

**Look & prompts**
- [ ] LOOK BLOCK per vehicle: lens + texture named (hardware, not
      vibes), three-layer depth, ONE motivated light, palette line
      (≤2 primaries + bone + ink, accent ≤10%), pose silhouette sentence,
      one motivated move, stability block in positive phrasing.
- [ ] THE LIGHTING BLOCK written once and pasted verbatim into every
      vehicle — no per-take rewording.
- [ ] Per-project BAN LIST written into the prompt pack (banned subjects,
      palettes, finishes, registers) alongside the standing anti-text
      clause in every singing vehicle.
- [ ] Every vehicle LEADS with its event sentence (peak on its named
      beat/word), then the LOOK block; every prompt is beat-by-beat
      action, not a situation; every 10 s take has motion planned across
      0–3 / 3–7 / 7–10 keyed to the song's events.
- [ ] Shot-list format: each vehicle line carries time range, framing,
      camera move, action, lyric, AND one line of background motion
      ("so the world always feels alive") (TechHalla).
- [ ] Singing vehicles follow performance-window staging (facing camera,
      static, MCU-or-closer during the line; gesture/camera strictly
      before the first word or after the last); singer changes only at
      phrase boundaries, reached via look → cut → reaction, with the
      listener's reaction written as its own planned slot.

**References & continuity**
- [ ] Reference stack generated per project (start/end pair per scene
      from the project's own material — never one anchor still reused
      across scenes); every vehicle names the stack frames it uses.
- [ ] Hero slots (S01, chorus peaks, chain first-frames) are STILL-FIRST:
      still generated, eye-gated, filed; video prompt carries behaviour
      only.
- [ ] Chain contract decided per sequence: resume-chain / two-image
      chain / portal chain with universal-joint heads and tails; portal
      joints get THE RADIANT PORTAL light logic.
- [ ] Category figures (if any) specified as READY-MADE FIGURES (type +
      prop kit + finish); named singers on identity lock.

**Fire order**
- [ ] CHORUS-HOOK TEST first: volley 1 leads with the hook scene; if its
      QC fails the plan's look, fix the PLAN before firing the rest.
      Never fire a whole song on an unproven look.
- [ ] Riskiest event included in the pilot volley when budget allows —
      learn early whether the signature happening is stageable.
- [ ] VOLUME-THEN-ESCALATE where a section needs options: cheap fast
      passes to lock acting/timing (DRAFT MODE), flagship spend only on
      the keepers (FINISH PASS); quiet/dark rejects are kept as tension
      before a hit — nothing wasted (TechHalla).
- [ ] Volley manifest stated before firing (scenes, takes owed, what
      each fixes); ≤6 videos per volley; VOLLEY_PROGRESS + TAKE_HISTORY
      updated after every volley before anything else fires.

**QC readiness**
- [ ] Gates staffed: G1–G7 (incl. G7 plan-sourced word sets), event-read
      test per scene, transformation timing read (cut on the peak, not
      the settle), G6 counted rules (vantage ≤10 s, collision quota,
      mouth-lie = fail), Rule-of-Six triage read per section (any
      section ≤2 on emotion fails even if every numeric gate passes).
- [ ] Zero-still / motion audit method chosen; mouth-openness (never
      frame-diff) is the AV-sync feature.
