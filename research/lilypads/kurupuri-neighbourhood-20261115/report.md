# Creator sweep — Kurupuri's neighbourhood (surreal / grotesque AI short video)

- **Seed:** user order, Telegram msg 428, 2026-11-15 — "now that you know the subject, you can find a bunch of other creators that do it." The subject = Kurupuri's territory: surreal/grotesque AI short video — body-horror transformations, weirdcore micro-animations, esoteric AI visuals that grab attention in seconds. Territory definition and format taken from the completed Kurupuri study (`lilypads/kurupuri0-20261115/report.md`).
- **Sweep date:** 2026-11-15.
- **Method:** Kevin-Bacon outward from three seed sets — (1) The LoopHole Project roster (theloopholeproject.ai, 177 creators, full list + film timestamps pulled), (2) #Weirdcore / #AIart / #AIanimation community search across YouTube and the open web, (3) Kurupuri's exhibition neighbourhoods (Fellowship Daily Program Seasons #2/#3 on daily.xyz, objkt). x.com fetch is policy-blocked and Xitter is down (user confirmed), so X-only creators are banked at roster/search level and marked as such. **Evidence levels are marked per creator: EVIDENCED = work/style confirmed from a reachable source beyond a name on a list; ROSTER-LEVEL / LEAD = name + handle confirmed, work not yet directly examined.** No audience numbers, tools, or works are claimed beyond what the cited sources show.

### From Kurupuri's exhibition neighbourhood — Fellowship Daily Program (seed 3)

**8. Taketo Muroi — daily.xyz / fellowship.xyz (Daily Program Season #2). EVIDENCED. ★ top-3 study queue**
A Daily Program Season #2 artist, in the same curated AI-video program as Kurupuri. Two named series, both dead-centre in the territory: **"Glitch as a Body"** (33+ numbered works) and **"Wall is Liquid"** — the latter with a published artist statement: the series explores "the ambiguity of the boundaries we take for granted, between reality and fiction, and between the visible and the invisible," aiming to visualise "the cognitive dissonance that occurs the moment the brain judges something as 'non-existent,' even though it is visually complete" — a self-described **"perceptual verification device"** designed to induce a "Cognitive Glitch." Signature move: the solid world quietly failing — walls turning liquid, the body as glitch — wrongness you perceive before you can name it. That is exactly the seconds-fast attention grab the territory runs on, produced by boundary-violation rather than gore.

**9. Olga Fedorova — daily.xyz Season #2 ("Broken Party," "Running after you"); NFTs on objkt + Foundation. EVIDENCED.**
Brussels-based artist (b. 1980, M.A. painting ENSAV) with a long pre-AI digital practice: 3D-rendered scenes of emotionless mannequin-like women in aseptic, clinical spaces — described by her gallery press as "deeply unsettling cyber women/mannequins," absurdist scenarios in "surreal, dystopian presentation [that] evoke uneasy, dreamlike states." She carried that uncanny-figure vocabulary into AI video in the Daily Program. Signature move: the too-still, too-smooth female figure in a sterile room — uncanny by restraint, the opposite pole from Kurupuri's fleshy excess, same unease. In-territory via the uncanny figure and the objkt overlap with Kurupuri's own marketplace.

### From the weirdcore / surreal AI-video community (seed 2)

**10. surreailist (Lukas Nowacki) — Instagram @surreailist, surreailist.art. EVIDENCED. ★ top-3 study queue**
Posts short AI films he calls **"psychological descents"** — profiled 2026-11-14 by The Daring Creatives as looking "like water-damaged folk horror somebody found in a Polish attic." Over 200 of them: each a short vertical loop with a two-word title and no explanation attached. The Berlin Music Video Awards put him on their 2026 jury, describing his work as breaking traditional narrative. He is unusually open about process (a free Patreon explaining "the what, the why and the how") and credits his tool by name: **almost exclusively Seedance 2.5** (sometimes 2.0), run through the Kinovi platform. Signature move: the unexplained folk-horror loop — dread with the exposition amputated; the loop is "the trailer, not the film." The closest living relative to Kurupuri's caption-less burst discipline found in this sweep, with one huge advantage: he documents his workflow.

**11. Niceaunties (Wenhui Lim) — niceaunties.com, @niceaunties. EVIDENCED. ★ top-3 study queue**
Singapore artist/designer (19 years in architecture) building the **Auntieverse** since January 2023: surreal AI video and installation centred on older women in imagined worlds of food, beauty, care and labour — hyperreal, poppy, funny-grotesque rather than horror-grotesque. Serious reach: TED talk Vancouver 2024, AI Action Summit Paris 2025, Louisiana Museum, V&A, Christie's Art+Tech, a solo show (*Auntiescapes*, Load Gallery Barcelona, May–Aug 2026). Her workflow is documented in unusual detail (The Daring Creatives, 2026-07-31): **image first, then teach it to move** — "I always start with the image… I make an image, and then I animate it, and then a sequence of this footage comes together to become a video," at **25–30 prompts per finished video**; tools she credits: Midjourney and DALL·E 3 (stills), Magnific (upscale), Runway and Pika (animation), **Suno (music)**. Signature move: one recurring cast (the aunties) inside an ever-expanding single universe — Kurupuri's "one coherent elsewhere" discipline at exhibition scale, with a music-integrated pipeline.

**12. Doopiidoo — YouTube / Instagram @doopiidoo. EVIDENCED.**
Russian AI artist based in Serbia; one of the eight artists in MikeTeevee's HUMANCENTRIC experiment (eight artists reinterpreting one Filippou script). Makes avant-garde surreal AI films **with his own music** — YouTube credits read like a WuBu intake sheet: "Nursed on Birch Milk" (Midjourney × Hailuo AI × Kling × Suno), "Heavy Nap" (Hailuo AI × Sora × Suno), concept/art direction/editing/post all his. Signature move: the self-scored surreal short — sound and image grown together rather than picture cut to a finished track. Directly relevant to a Suno-first MV pipeline: he is the rare surrealist who composes for his own morphs.

**13. Allanweird (+ Rocket) — slopart.group, Costa Rica. EVIDENCED.**
Founder of the **SLOP ART MOVEMENT (SAM)** — a manifesto practice reclaiming "AI slop" as art, co-credited with Rocket, his ChatGPT counterpart. The works are surreal video loops of exactly Kurupuri's length class: "The Lost Limits of the Cro-Magnon Woman" (7.667s loop — a persistent fly interrupting two Renaissance-style sitters; image via ChatGPT, animation via Runway), and "Your Mother Is Not My Father" — **a looping video of a man cradling an enormous ear in a supermarket** while a woman reads a tin: mundane retail space + one grotesque oversized body-object, the loop hidden in the cradling motion. That is Kurupuri's office-invasion grammar independently reinvented, with the loop seam deliberately concealed. Covered by Urgent Matter (Oct 2026). Signature move: the deadpan grotesque loop with the seam hidden in a repeated gesture.

**14. David Szauder — davidszauder / TED. EVIDENCED (lighter touch).**
Hungarian AI surrealist; TED talk "Welcome to My AI Surrealist World" (TEDNext 2025): "strange, extravagantly dressed characters live in an ever-changing opera" — a digitally rewired dreamscape / inner theatre. Also one of the eight HUMANCENTRIC artists. Signature move: the perpetual costume-opera — character pageantry as the surreal engine. Least grotesque name on this list; banked as the community's bridge figure between gallery surrealism and AI film, and as a second Kevin-Bacon hop into the HUMANCENTRIC eight (which also holds Doopiidoo, above, and Mindeye/Veronika Pell, not banked here).

## Next studies — the queue (top 3)

1. **surreailist (Lukas Nowacki)** — *Question: how does he sustain dread in a sub-20-second vertical loop with zero exposition — what are the loop-closure, two-word-title, and sound disciplines, and what does his documented Seedance-2.5 process do that our takes don't?* He is the only creator in the sweep who both works Kurupuri's exact format (caption-less micro-loop) and publishes his how. Direct bumper-craft transfer.
2. **Taketo Muroi** — *Question: how is the "cognitive glitch" engineered — what does the frame look like at the exact moment a solid thing reads as liquid/impossible, and can that single-frame wrongness be prompted reliably as a bumper's peak frame?* His own statement names the mechanism (visually complete but judged non-existent); a study should extract the visual recipe behind "Wall is Liquid" / "Glitch as a Body" and test it against our frame-0 = peak-image rule.
3. **Niceaunties** — *Question: what does still-first production (25–30 prompts, image → animate → sequence) do to morph/identity stability versus our text-first takes — and how does a Suno-scored surreal universe stay one universe across hundreds of pieces?* The pipeline A/B is testable on our own stack, and the Auntieverse is the best-documented case of the "one coherent elsewhere" discipline Kurupuri's study already flagged.

Runners-up when the rotation reaches them: Doopiidoo (self-scored surreal shorts — the Suno question), Allanweird (hidden loop seams), then the roster-level LoopHole names in film order, starting with Mystique Licorne's 02:52 segment.
