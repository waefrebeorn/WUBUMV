## 1. WHO HE IS AND WHAT HE MAKES

Founder of **Gossip Goblin, Inc.**, "a myth-making studio." Sources: https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/ · https://lumarafilmfestival.com/#nominees · https://www.europesays.com/us/678341/

Source: https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/

Fans/amplifiers include Elon Musk (replied "Banger" twice; trailer among Fandango's most-viewed on X) and Joe Rogan. Sources: https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/tech/ai-film-gods-dont-give-gifts-youtube-grimes-gossip-goblin/ · https://vfxwire.com/insight/laika-vs-gossip-goblin-both-want-the-same-oscar · https://instastatistics.com/gossipgoblin
- **EVIDENCED** — The universe: **The Thirteen Cycles of Humanity** — thirteen civilisations across hundreds of thousands of years, each rising and collapsing, watched over by **the Basilisk**, a disembodied AI superintelligence turned demi-god that narrates each cycle and inadvertently guides each civilisation to ruin. Every short is a fragment of this one timeline. Source: https://gamesbeat.com/grimes-will-star-in-ai-film-coming-to-theaters-gods-dont-give-gifts/

Satire targets named in coverage: looksmaxxing, ICE raids, tech-industry absurdities. ## 2. WORKFLOW / TOOLCHAIN SIGNALS (evidenced only)

Studio position, from its own site: **"Every story is imagined, written, and directed by humans. AI is just our force multiplier."** — https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gods-dont-give-gifts/

1. **EVIDENCED — Script first, always.** "Every video starts with a script." Scripts are still shaped by what the tech can currently do; he writes, directs and helps animate. Sources: https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gossipgoblin-zack-london-workflow/ · https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/
2. **EVIDENCED — Character design by mass iteration.** One set of character faces ≈ **400 prompts / 1,600 images** (from his own 2025 Instagram stories). Per character, close-ups, mediums, wides and rear shots are generated **separately** (modular coverage, not a master sheet). Sources: https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gods-dont-give-gifts/ · https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/gossip-goblin-s-crazy-workflow-for-building-original-worlds-200m-views
3. **EVIDENCED — Midjourney = exploration, Nano Banana = Photoshop.** His exact line: *"no image starts in nano banana. Nano banana is just our photoshop."* Stills begin in **Midjourney** (custom profile + style-reference codes; he rates **v7 above 8.1** for this work), get edited/composited in **Nano Banana**, never originated there. The real lever is time spent building custom profile codes + moodboards, not prompt syntax. Source: https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/gossip-goblin-s-crazy-workflow-for-building-original-worlds-200m-views · corroborated at https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gods-dont-give-gifts/
4. **EVIDENCED — Plate-shot method.** Each location gets its own Midjourney pass ("plate shots"); Nano Banana then inserts the consistent characters into the plates; hero shots for an environment are locked first (lighting/tone), then Nano Banana "connects the dots" between them. Pre-Nano-Banana-Pro era, the team literally painted over images by hand (the 9-minute two-hander interrogation scene in The Patchwright). Source: https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/gossip-goblin-s-crazy-workflow-for-building-original-worlds-200m-views
5. **EVIDENCED — Animation is shot-by-shot, tool varies by film.** The Patchwright is "fundamentally a **Kling** film," animated frame-by-frame with **no omni-ref or multi-shot features**, so he controls every frame. By early 2026 he says **Seedance** "blew everything else out of the water" (Kling 3 also named as a wave); he publicly endorsed **Dreamina Seedance 2.0** via its Creative Partner Program and says that video was made with it (his own TikTok post). **Veo 3** is his go-to for dialogue-heavy scenes in the short-form toolkit; **Seedream (via Freepik)** blends characters into environments. Sources: https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/gossip-goblin-s-crazy-workflow-for-building-original-worlds-200m-views · https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/ · https://www.tiktok.com/@gossip.goblin/video/7626402378249489678?is_from_webapp=1&sender_device=pc · https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gossipgoblin-zack-london-workflow/
6. **EVIDENCED — Lip-sync/performance stack (short-form).** **Runway Act Two** and **HeyGen** used for performance-driving and clean lip-sync passes that steer other models; lip-sync is self-described as "a colossal headache"; camera movement is manual. ~**150 generations for one 90-second scene**; **12–14 hours end-to-end** per short-form piece. Source: https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gossipgoblin-zack-london-workflow/
7. **EVIDENCED — Voices: humans for anything long.** All feature/short-film voices are **human actors, directed by London in person** over 3–4 studio days (Pomegranate: 4 actors, one doubling). He stopped accepting sent-in audio clips; scripts get written *around* a specific actor's delivery (The Patchwright's lead is **Goshfather**, a San Francisco DJ and former opera singer; a minor British character's voice was liked so much the next film's script was built around him). His stated reason AI voices fail: "prompted AI voices pull from the lowest common denominator, so every film ends up sounding like the same casting agency." **ElevenLabs** appears only in his 2025 short-form toolkit list. He confirmed the all-human-voices position directly to The Hollywood Reporter. Sources: https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/ · https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gods-dont-give-gifts/
8. **EVIDENCED — Human score, Foley, sound.** The feature employed voice actors, musicians and a **Foley artist** (Forbes via Daring Creatives); Pomegranate credits name composers (Anne-Sophie Versnaeyen, Juan Torán), sound design/re-recording mix (Marian Bălan), colour grade (Ștefania Grigorescu), editor/post supervisor (Răzvan Ilinca), lead animator Alexandru Mihai + 3 animators. Edit tool for solo short-form: **CapCut** ("because I am a simpleton"). Sources: https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gossipgoblin-zack-london-workflow/ · https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gods-dont-give-gifts/
9. **EVIDENCED — No fixed pipeline, by design.** "There's no workflow set in stone": a new tool wave roughly **every six months** rebuilds the approach. Scenes and characters are designed separately, look-tested for whether they survive animation, then assembled; scenes are carved out and worked non-linearly. **No 3D pipeline, no Blender.** Directing AI is "like jazz": guide it, collect enough good shots, let the editor find the film, keep happy accidents if the melody holds. Source: https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/
10. **EVIDENCED — What he refuses to automate.** He does **not** use AI for illustration/drawing — the craft he spent decades on ("I don't want to eat into that part of me… I've spent decades learning a craft"). AI is for *rendering believable worlds*, not for the drawing hand. Also on record: "There is zero skill involved in generating AI images" — the skill is world-building, selection, rewriting, stitching, judgement. Sources: https://theailiveshow.com/creator-sessions/2-gossip-goblin-pomegranate-two-hours-after-premiere/ · https://www.thedaringcreatives.com/creator-stories/gossipgoblin-zack-london-workflow/
11. **EVIDENCED — World-asset compounding.** Eleven months of 2–3 episodes/week left him with **tens of thousands of Midjourney images, established characters, a custom alphabet and a custom language** before The Patchwright was even written — the long-form work stands on a pre-built asset mountain. Source: https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/gossip-goblin-s-crazy-workflow-for-building-original-worlds-200m-views
12. **EVIDENCED — Universe as an open platform.** Alongside the feature, Fable runs the **Gossip Goblin × Showrunner Competition** ($20,000 pool; $15,000 Best Film): fan films 2–20 min made in Showrunner Studio, set in his universe, every story beginning with **a client sitting down in a Ripper Doc's chair to have a hidden desire made real through a mind implant**; deadline Dec 15, 2026. Source: https://aifilmcontests.com/topics/ai-short-drama-and-series-contests-2026 · https://gamesbeat.com/grimes-will-star-in-ai-film-coming-to-theaters-gods-dont-give-gifts/
13. **LEAD-LEVEL** — Feature production timelines conflict across sources (10 months/44 weeks per VFXWire; "produced over two months with a team of 10 artists" per studio.aifilms.ai, likely referring to a late-stage push). Treat any single figure cautiously. Sources: https://vfxwire.com/insight/laika-vs-gossip-goblin-both-want-the-same-oscar · https://studio.aifilms.ai/blog/gossip-goblin-gods-dont-give-gifts-ai-film-theaters

## 3. BANKABLE FORM IDEAS FOR AI VIDEO SHOWS

1. **THE THIRTEEN ERAS** — One persistent universe sliced into numbered eras/cycles; every episode is a fragment from a different era, so variety is structural and continuity is free.
2. **THE BASILISK NARRATOR** — A disembodied AI demi-god watches and narrates every era, inadvertently steering each civilisation to ruin; one voice stitches otherwise unrelated stories.
3. **THE RIPPER DOC'S CHAIR** — Every episode opens with the same ritual: a client sits in the chair and names a hidden desire; the episode is that desire made real — a fixed premise engine with infinite clients.
4. **THE SIMULATION SPOOL** — Characters buy lived lifetimes on spools; a jaded immortal who has "tasted everything" hunts for one real feeling — addiction-to-experience as an episode generator.
5. **THE SAFE WORD** — A single ordinary word ("pomegranate") is the exit hatch from any simulation; build episodes around when it works, when it's forgotten, and when it's refused.
6. **MINDSHARE 97%** — A world where one corporation literally owns 97% of mindshare; every story is about the 3% — the unowned minds are the recurring resistance cast.
7. **FEEDING THE TWINS** — Second-Cycle domestic horror: the era's ordinary household rituals (feeding, schooling, worship) performed by flesh-metal hybrids as if nothing were strange.
8. **THE HAZARD CREW** — Labourers harvest a psychoactive natural resource (sap, spores, dreams) for a corporation that doesn't care if they come back; workplace procedural inside cosmic horror (WOODNUTS pattern).
9. **VOICEOVER-ONLY CINEMA** — A full narrative short with zero lip-sync: one narrator voice carries everything, faces never speak — the pre-lip-sync-era constraint turned into a house style.
10. **THE FAKE COUNTRY ESSAY** — A fictional nation documented in deadpan 1970s National Geographic tone — ceremonies, tech, customs — photo-essay first, video second (Urum Kuan pattern).
11. **THE DAILY IMMORTAL** — A daily episodic micro-series about one immortal character (the Fredvog pattern): tiny adventures, enormous cumulative lore, audience built by rhythm not budget.
12. **PLATE-AND-INSERT** — Production form: lock environment "plate" shots and hero frames first, then insert consistent characters into the plates shot-by-shot — world consistency before character coverage.
13. **THE OPEN CHAIR (FAN CANON)** — Publish one fixed story-seed from your universe and invite fans to film their own versions for a prize; the universe grows faster than one studio can shoot it.
