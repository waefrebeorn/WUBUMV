## What he makes now — the feed practice

His 2026 feed (141 video posts in the read set) is dominated
by one repeatable format:

### 1. The "Scoring X" rescore reel (EVIDENCED, dominant format)
He takes **existing footage he did not shoot** — Mordhau and
Chivalry 2 gameplay, night-drive footage, fantasy/medieval
scenes — and **rescores it with his own composed track**,
posting the result as a short reel. The caption formula is
fixed and countable: a poetic one-liner plus *"Scoring the
[thing], [unit] by [unit]"* — e.g. 2026-11-13: "I used my
track Battle Of The Rebels to rescore this Mordhau and
Chivalry 2 gameplay, **shield by shield, charge by charge**";
others run "light by light, mile by mile", "sword by sword",
"wave by wave, color by color". The discipline the captions
advertise: the music is authored to the picture's visible
micro-beats, and the work is *counted* in those units. This
is a composer's portfolio practice run at feed cadence —
picture fixed first, music written to picture.

### 2. Adaptive / interactive audio reels (EVIDENCED)
Game-audio craft shown as a visible stack — e.g.
2026-08-25: *"Layers, one at a time: Music, footsteps,
weapons, hits, gear, efforts, the space around them."* The
reel builds the full mix layer by layer so the audience
hears (and sees) each stratum arrive.

## Took (mechanics only)

1. **THE RESCORE** — Lock the picture first; author the music
   layer to the picture's visible micro-beats, and *count the
   work in the picture's own units* (shield by shield, charge
   by charge). Brief-able mechanic for WuBu: for wordless
   slots and show units, generate/lock the visual loop FIRST,
   then select and trim the Suno section so its hits land on
   the loop's visible events — the inverse direction of the
   tempo gospel, legitimate exactly where there are no words
   to sync. Second use: an A/B practice — one locked piece of
   footage, two different scores, as an emotional-reading test
   before committing a build's track edit.
2. **THE LAYER STACK** — Build the slot as strata that arrive
   one at a time, each entrance locked to one stem's entrance:
   kick-only world → +bass object → +vocal figure → +space/
   atmosphere. Distinct from the banked stem→parameter
   modulation table (that maps stems onto *parameters of one
   scene*; this *adds scene strata* per stem). Serves
   instrumental slots, build-to-BLAM openings (the stack IS
   the ramp, but every layer lands as a hit, not a swell),
   and THE MACHINE's machine-waking units.
3. **THE SINGLE MODULATION RULE** (restraint rule, not a new
   scene form) — Per scene, exactly ONE stem drives exactly
   ONE visual parameter; everything else is held. This is
   Capuano's evidenced practitioner discipline inside Neural
   Frames (bass → Strength, nothing else moving). It sharpens
   the already-banked modulation table into a per-slot law:
   if two parameters are pulsing, neither reads.
4. **THE MUSIC-DRAWN LINE** — A code-rendered visual whose
   entire content is one continuous drawn mark (line, ribbon,
   contour) and whose *only* motion is caused by the music,
   drawn live from the stems — Capuano's self-built visualizer
   stated as a buildable form. Family G (code-rendered)
   adjacent, but the specific form — the drawing is the whole
   subject, no scene, no figure — is not in the catalogue.
   Serves bridges, instrumental breaks, and THE COUNT's
   counting-line devices.

Also noted, as titling discipline rather than a visual form:
his counted caption formula ("Scoring the X, noun by noun")
is a unit-naming habit — name each unit by its counted
micro-beats and the beat-map is written before the build
starts.
