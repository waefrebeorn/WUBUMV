composer, not another tool. Dom (Domenico) Capuano (b. 1975,

## 4. New visual forms — candidates for the catalogue

Checked against `~/workspace/custom-skills/visual-mv-forms/SKILL.md`
(families A–I and its named forms: THE POSE-CUT, LIGHT-SHIFT
SECTION CHANGE, THE LOOPHOLE, THE KEYHOLE, THE FOG SEAM, THE
GAG-TRANSFORMATION, THE LIQUID WALL, THE VERIFICATION FREEZE)
and against form ideas banked in studies/INDEX but not in the
skill file (THE ANCHOR, THE CRADLE SHOT, THE SATELLITE ORBIT,
THE HARBOR, THE RIVER, THE FEATHER, THE SAILBOAT, THE SUN, THE
LADDER, THE VESSEL, THE GESTURE SEAM, THE DECOY READER, THE
LULLABY LOOP, THE PERSISTENT FLY, STILL-FIRST SLOT, ONE
COHERENT ELSEWHERE, THE STERILE ROOM, THE LENTICULAR CUT, THE
READY-MADE FIGURE, ONE TITLE TWO RELEASES, PHRASE-LENGTH
TAKES, THE PLACE BECOMES THE CREATURE, THE LIFECYCLE ARC, THE
SYNOPSIS POEM, THE LIGHTING BLOCK, THE RADIANT PORTAL, THE
SOFT THREAT). All six below are genuinely new against both
lists.

1. **THE RESCORE** — *Mechanic:* lock the picture first; author
   the music/sound layer to the picture's visible micro-beats,
   and count the work in the picture's own units (a duel =
   shield by shield, charge by charge). *Seen at:* Dom
   Capuano's dominant 2026 feed format (Mordhau/Chivalry 2
   gameplay, night drives rescored with his own tracks).
   *Serves:* wordless MV slots and show units where the tempo
   gospel has no words to serve — picture-first slots in THE
   MACHINE; also an A/B method (one locked visual, two scores)
   for choosing a track edit before assembly.
2. **THE LAYER STACK** — *Mechanic:* the scene is built as
   strata that arrive one at a time, each entrance locked to
   one stem's entrance (kick-only world → +bass object →
   +vocal figure → +space). Capuano's own demo names the audio
   version: "Music, footsteps, weapons, hits, gear, efforts,
   the space around them." Distinct from the banked
   stem→parameter table: parameters modulate one scene; strata
   *add* the scene. *Seen at:* Capuano's adaptive-audio reels
   (2026-08-25). *Serves:* instrumental slots, build-to-BLAM
   openings (every layer lands as a hit, never a swell), THE
   MACHINE's machine-waking units. Variant (from Davvoz's
   Aurora Player): one visual *mode* per stem — orb /
   kaleidoscope / particle field crossfading as stems enter.
3. **THE MUSIC-DRAWN LINE** — *Mechanic:* a code-rendered
   visual whose entire content is one continuous drawn mark
   (line, ribbon, contour) and whose only motion is caused by
   the music, drawn live from the stems — no scene, no figure.
   *Seen at:* Capuano's self-built visualizer (his caption,
   2026-08-18). *Serves:* bridges and instrumental breaks in
   MVs; THE COUNT's counting-line device; any slot needing
   motion with zero identity-drift risk (there is nothing in
   frame that *can* drift).
4. **THE MODULATION PASS** — *Mechanic:* a two-stage build —
   generate the take for motion in stage one, then run a
   second, stem-reactive modulation pass in post that
   re-authors the take's pulse (strength, zoom, colour
   pressure) to the actual song stems. *Seen at:* Ben Nash's
   reported stack (elements generated in Moon Valley, then
   modulated in Neural Frames). *Serves:* WuBu's existing
   takes whose motion is right but whose pulse ignores the
   track — a post-stage fix that doesn't refire the take; the
   natural home is our own C11/code layer, not their platform.
5. **THE ONE-HUE WORLD** — *Mechanic:* an entire episode or
   video obeys a single dominant-hue law — every unit's world,
   wardrobe and props conform to one colour signature; the hue
   *is* the identity, held across every cut. Distinct from
   Method C's per-show graphic accent colours (those dress the
   graphics layer; this law binds the whole photographed
   image). *Seen at:* mirali6is — "AI with a RED attitude",
   a 2,000+ post catalogue under one declared hue.
   *Serves:* new-era show episodes needing cross-unit identity
   that survives cast drift (a PILOT-3-class problem): faces
   may wobble, the hue law cannot.
6. **THE CANON REMIX** — *Mechanic:* take a canonical,
   instantly recognised public-domain structure (a Bach cello
   line, a Sarabande) and re-render it wholesale in the build's
   contemporary electronic idiom — recognition is pre-loaded,
   the idiom swap is the event, and the first second hooks on
   familiarity rather than novelty. *Seen at:* Capuano's
   classical rescored reels (Bach in neon; Sarabande as analog
   synthwave). *Serves:* MV hooks and THE MACHINE's aired-video
   slots. **Guardrail: public-domain material only** — the
   standing no-licensed-material rule is untouched; the canon
   here is centuries old, not a licensed property.

Also recorded, as a rule rather than a form: **THE SINGLE
MODULATION RULE** (Capuano) — per scene, exactly one stem
drives exactly one visual parameter; proposed as a law inside
the already-banked stem→parameter modulation table.
