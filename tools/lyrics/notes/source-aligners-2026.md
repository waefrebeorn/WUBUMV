# Forced aligners 2026
- Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B HF model card, read live 2026-11-10: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B-hf
  Released 29 Jan 2026 with Qwen3-ASR 0.6B/1.7B. NAR, single forward pass, up to 5 min speech, 11 langs (zh,en,yue,fr,de,it,ja,ko,pt,ru,es). Accepts transcripts from any ASR (example uses Parakeet CTC). Qwen claims ~42.9ms avg error vs 129.8ms NeMo FA and 133.2ms WhisperX; human-labelled 32.4ms (per TranscribeX summary of Qwen claims — vendor numbers).
  Qwen3-ASR model card audio types: "Speech, Singing Voice, Songs with BGM" — https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-hf
- Where Words Begin and End, arXiv 2610.17782v2, Oct 2026, read live: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17782v2 and summary https://scicom.app/newsroom/where-words-begin-and-end
  Pilot (defaults, TIMIT clean, speech not singing): MFA 3.4.2 arpa 19.1ms MAE, 68.2% within 20ms, RTF 0.021 on CPU; Qwen3-FA 30.1ms clean, 52.7ms at 8kHz; WhisperX 37.2ms on FA-Bench TIMIT (table) / 77ms in another table in noise-robust paper — different protocols, do not mix. FA-Bench TIMIT: released Whisper-Scicom LoRA aligner 11.6-11.7ms, MFA 21.8ms, Qwen 32.2ms, WhisperX 37.2ms. Scicom models CC BY-NC 4.0 at https://huggingface.co/collections/Scicom-intl/force-alignment — note NC licence.
  CTC aligners start words late: median first char 33ms after human start, last char 26ms before end; 67% of frames inside a word are blank.
  MFA needs dictionary + acoustic model per language, fails ~1/5 utterances on FLEURS per-language run; singing: 2024 study open MFA model only 10.08% high-accuracy on singing, improved after singing training.
- WhisperX 3.8.6 (25 May 2026), faster-whisper + wav2vec2 alignment + pyannote diarization. Paper: 93.2% vs 85.4% precision at 200ms vs Whisper. ELYSY: CPU ~3-8 min/song, GPU 30-90s, ~3.5GB models. https://gabrielx.com/elysy-elizs-lyrics-synchronizer/ (index) and https://www.forasoft.com/learn/ai-for-video-engineering/articles-ai/whisperx-diarization-word-level-timestamps (index)
- Nightingale docs, read live: https://nightingale.cafe/docs/lyrics/ — production karaoke app pattern: LRCLIB first, else Demucs/isolated vocals -> WhisperX large-v3 or Parakeet v3 (CPU/MPS via onnx-asr, CUDA via NeMo), fallback Whisper if Parakeet yields nothing; alignment backends WhisperX wav2vec2 (default, Python Viterbi on CPU), torchaudio forced_align GPU/CPU kernel, or Qwen3-ForcedAligner (~1.8GB, 11 langs, up to ~5min/pass, falls back to wav2vec2).
- CrisperWhisper 2.0 (PyPI, index 2026-11-10): https://pypi.org/project/crisperwhisper/0.1.2/ — ~30ms mean boundary error read speech, 41ms conversational, verbatim mode, seamless longform via conditional continuation. Speech-focused, not proven on singing; 1.0 was English/German only.
- whisper-timestamped / stable-ts: DTW over Whisper cross-attention, 20ms grid; superseded for precision by forced aligners. No 2026 maintenance evidence found.
